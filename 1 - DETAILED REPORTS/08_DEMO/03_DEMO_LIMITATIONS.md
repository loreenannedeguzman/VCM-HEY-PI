# Demo Limitations

## Measurement Boundary

A live demo is an operational demonstration, not a substitute for the final benchmark record. The canonical benchmark values remain in 05_RESULTS.

## Known Limits

- Final benchmark evidence uses a 20-trial population rather than broad population statistics.
- Broad environmental wake FAR is not established.
- Full acoustic speaker-onset response latency is not established.
- E37's exact final training-row membership is not fully reconstructed.
- E53/VCM2 is independent and is not part of the E50 demo path.

## Safety Boundary

Only accepted commands should route to local actions. Rejected, unknown, or low-confidence cases should produce no unsafe action under the documented policy.
