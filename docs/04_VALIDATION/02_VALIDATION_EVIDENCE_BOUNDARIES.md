# Validation Evidence Boundaries

## Established

- The final benchmark used the frozen E37/E50/E40 core.
- The 20-trial population contains 19 valid command trials plus one UNKNOWN/no-action trial.
- Wake, acceptance, action precision, safe rejection, return-to-listening, latency, RTF, and bounded command-window FAR values are reported in 05_RESULTS.
- E37 Hey Pi recording evidence exists and is documented by the recovered Pi manifest.

## Not Established

- A full broad-environment wake false-accept-rate study.
- Full acoustic speaker-onset response latency.
- Formal per-speaker generalization statistics for every command.
- Exact final E37 training-row membership.
- E37 training epochs, optimizer, validation metrics, augmentation count, and complete speaker/recordist count.

## Evidence Separation

E37 wake-recording evidence is separate from E50 command-model dataset provenance. The recovered E37 recording set is not classified as collective Gold Dataset material, and it is not part of the E50 16,100-row command-model training manifest.
