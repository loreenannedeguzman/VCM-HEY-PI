# E53 and E50 Separation

## Rule

E53/VCM2 is not part of the frozen E50 delivery stack. It must remain a separate independent experiment.

## Practical Consequences

- Do not include E53 data in E50's 16,100-row training manifest.
- Do not use E53 metrics as E50 benchmark results.
- Do not describe E53 as replacing E50.
- Do not use E53 to establish E50 deployment, routing, DUi behavior, or Pi benchmark claims.
- Do not merge E53 chronology into the E50 engineering history.

## Correct Framing

E53 can be described as an independent experiment using selected material from the broader collective/Dataset2 family with its own project-specific construction. It does not change the frozen E50 core.
